# Kioku — Privacy policy

*Last updated: 22 November 2026*

Kioku is a flashcard app for learning Japanese.

**Kioku collects no personal data.** It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't connect to the internet.

- Your study progress, settings and any cards you create (including photos you attach) are stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage.
- If you use **Export backup**, the app writes a file to the location you choose. Kioku never sends it anywhere.
- The ▶ sound buttons use your phone's own text-to-speech engine. Kioku passes it only the Japanese text being read aloud.
- Android's automatic backup may copy the app's data to your Google account if you have turned that on in your phone's settings. That is controlled by Android and Google, not by Kioku.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your device.
